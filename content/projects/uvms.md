## What it is

An underwater vehicle-manipulator system: a BlueROV2 Heavy carrying a Reach Robotics
Alpha 5 arm, with an end-effector trajectory-tracking framework — the vehicle and the arm
move together so the gripper follows a commanded path. Published work with paper and video.

## The pieces

- **`uvms`** — the tracking framework (paper code).
- **`uvms_msgs`**, **`alpha_msgs`** — the interfaces for the framework and the arm.
