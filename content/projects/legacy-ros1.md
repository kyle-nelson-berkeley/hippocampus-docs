## What it is

The pre-ROS 2 HippoCampus software stack, preserved. The lab moved to ROS 2; these repos
stay readable for anyone revisiting old experiments or wondering why something is shaped
the way it is.

## The pieces

`hippocampus_common`, `hippocampus_msgs`, `hippocampus_sim`, `control`, `multi_uuv`
(multi-vehicle experiments), and `howto` (old setup notes).

## Where it stands

Archive. Do not build new work on these. The old site's deprecated ROS 1 pages were
deliberately not migrated to this site — the reasons are recorded in the parity table
(`docs/setup-parity.md` in this site's repository), and the old pages remain readable on
the old site.
